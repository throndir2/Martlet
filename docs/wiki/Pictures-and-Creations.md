# Pictures and Creations

Martlet stores things it makes as **creations**: pictures, songs and reports.

## Pictures

Open **Companion › Pictures**. Choose where it draws:

| Place | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Martlet's Pictures role** | ComfyUI with Z-Image Turbo on this PC or a paired NVIDIA host. |
| **My own ComfyUI** | Your existing ComfyUI on your network. |
| **OpenRouter** | Cloud image API; costs money. |
| **NVIDIA Build** | NVIDIA FLUX models with an `nvapi-` key. |

Use **Check** to test readiness. **Draw a test picture** draws one image; cloud choices ask first.

## Conversation

When Pictures is set up and Thinking supports tools, ask "draw me a fox curled up in the snow." Martlet starts a background job, keeps chatting, then shows the image and saves a `picture` creation.

## Creations page

Open **Creations** to see kind, title, size, date, where it is stored, summary, text/details, **Rename** and **Delete**. There is no Play button; ask Martlet to sing or show creations.

## Sync and safety

Creations sync through paired hosts while shared settings are on. Do not generate illegal/harmful content or real people without consent; disclose AI-generated images when sharing.
