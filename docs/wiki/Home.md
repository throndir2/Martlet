Martlet is a Windows desktop AI companion. You talk or type; it replies in a chosen voice while a Live2D or VRM character speaks on your desktop with speech bubbles.

It can listen, recognize voices, watch your screen or a camera, remember facts, use tools, draw pictures, write and sing songs, connect to Home Assistant, Discord and Telegram, and share work across your own computers.

Latest release: **v0.48.0**. Download `Martlet-<version>-win-x64.exe` from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/throndir2/Martlet/releases/latest). It installs per user on Windows 10 2004+ or Windows 11 x64. The installer is unsigned; Windows may show an unknown-publisher warning.
