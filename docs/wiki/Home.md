## Home in the app

The app's **Home** page shows setup steps, health checks and items that need attention. Its **Get started** card guides you through three steps:

1. Set up where Martlet runs.
2. Choose who Martlet is.
3. Talk with Martlet.

Home also opens the controls for each step. See [First Steps](First-Steps).
