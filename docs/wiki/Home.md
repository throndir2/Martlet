Martlet is an AI companion that lives on your desktop. Talk to her or type, and she answers out loud in the voice you chose. Her Live2D or VRM character stands on top of your windows, moves her lips while she talks and shows each sentence in a speech bubble.

She can listen hands-free, know who is talking, watch your game with you, read up on the games you play, remember what matters to you, react when you touch her, draw pictures, write and sing songs, use tools, control your smart home and chat on Discord, Telegram and WhatsApp. You choose where her mind runs: on this PC, on your other computers or with a cloud AI. Martlet is free, and no sign-up is needed.

[![Latest release](https://img.shields.io/github/v/release/throndir2/Martlet?style=flat-square&color=c2185b&label=latest)](https://github.com/throndir2/Martlet/releases/latest)

Download `Martlet-<version>-win-x64.exe` from [the latest release](https://github.com/throndir2/Martlet/releases/latest). It installs for your Windows user on Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) or Windows 11, 64-bit. The installer is not code-signed, so Windows can ask you to confirm before it runs. An early desktop app for Linux and Mac is in each release too.
