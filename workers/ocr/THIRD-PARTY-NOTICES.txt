Third-party components used by the Martlet OCR worker images (host/Dockerfile for the rapidocr engine, host/Dockerfile.ppocrv5 for the ppocrv5 engine). They are installed at build time from PyPI, and the PP-OCRv5 models are downloaded at build time. None are stored in this repository.

RapidOCR 1.4.4 and rapidocr_onnxruntime 1.4.4
  https://github.com/RapidAI/RapidOCR
  License: Apache-2.0
  Includes PaddleOCR PP-OCRv4 ONNX models.
  PaddleOCR and PP-OCRv4 license: Apache-2.0

RapidOCR 3.10.0 (ppocrv5 engine)
  https://github.com/RapidAI/RapidOCR
  License: Apache-2.0

PaddleOCR PP-OCRv5 mobile and server ONNX models (ppocrv5 engine)
  https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR
  Downloaded from RapidOCR's ModelScope release v3.10.0 (https://www.modelscope.cn/models/RapidAI/RapidOCR) or an identical Hugging Face copy, each SHA-256 checked.
  License: Apache-2.0

ONNX Runtime 1.30.0 (rapidocr engine) and 1.31.0 (ppocrv5 engine; onnxruntime-gpu 1.31.0 with the gpu accelerator)
  https://onnxruntime.ai
  License: MIT
